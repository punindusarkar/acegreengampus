"Holy Basil, commonly known as Tulsi, is a highly revered plant in India and Southeast Asia, known for its medicinal, religious, and environmental benefits. It belongs to the Lamiaceae family and is a small, aromatic herb with green or purple leaves and tiny white or purple flowers. Tulsi is a hardy plant that grows well in warm climates with moderate water needs. Holy Basil is widely used in Ayurvedic medicine due to its powerful antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. It is commonly consumed as tea or herbal extract to boost immunity, improve digestion, and relieve respiratory issues like coughs and colds. It also helps in reducing stress, regulating blood sugar, and supporting heart health. Besides its medicinal properties, Tulsi is sacred in Hinduism, often planted near temples and homes for spiritual and environmental benefits. It is believed to purify the surroundings and promote well-being. The plant plays an important environmental role by absorbing carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen, making it an excellent air purifier. Additionally, it acts as a natural mosquito repellent. With its vast health benefits, Holy Basil remains a vital part of traditional and modern herbal medicine."
