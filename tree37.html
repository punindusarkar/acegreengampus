"Dieffenbachia seguine, commonly known as Dumb Cane or Leopard Lily, is a herbaceous perennial plant belonging to the Araceae family. It is native to South America and the Caribbean and is widely grown as a decorative indoor plant due to its large, variegated green and creamy-white leaves. The plant thrives in indirect light and prefers moist but well-drained soil with a humid environment. Although Dieffenbachia seguine produces small, greenish-white flowers and orange to red berries, flowering is rare indoors. The plant is known for its air-purifying abilities, helping to remove toxins like formaldehyde and benzene, thereby improving indoor air quality. However, it is highly toxic to humans and pets due to calcium oxalate crystals in its sap, which can cause severe irritation, swelling, and temporary speech loss if ingested—hence the name ""Dumb Cane."" Despite its toxicity, the Dieffenbachia is popular for its resilience and ease of care. It can grow up to 10 feet tall and is propagated through stem cuttings. With its attractive foliage and low maintenance needs, it remains a favorite houseplant for both homes and offices. Proper handling and placement away from pets and children are essential for safe cultivation."
