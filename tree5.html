"**Ixora coccinea**, commonly known as Jungle Geranium, Flame of the Woods, or West Indian Jasmine, is a vibrant flowering shrub belonging to the Rubiaceae family. Native to South Asia, it is widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions for its ornamental value. The plant is known for its dense clusters of small, tubular flowers that bloom in striking colors like red, orange, pink, yellow, and white. Its glossy, leathery leaves are simple and elliptical, adding to its aesthetic appeal. Ixora is a low-maintenance shrub that thrives in full sun or partial shade with regular watering and well-drained soil. It is popular in gardens, hedges, and landscapes, as it flowers year-round and attracts pollinators like butterflies and bees, promoting biodiversity. The plant also has traditional medicinal uses. Its flowers and leaves are known for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, and it has been used in treating fever, skin infections, wounds, and dysentery in folk medicine. Beyond its beauty, Ixora contributes to the environment by absorbing carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen, making it an eco-friendly choice for landscaping. Compact, resilient, and colorful, it is a favorite for enhancing gardens and creating lush, vibrant spaces."
